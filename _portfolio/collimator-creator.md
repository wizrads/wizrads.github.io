An open-source tool for automated, parametric creation of custom collimators for preclinical irradiation studies, removing the manual CAD step from FLASH and small-animal experiment setup.

Presented at AAPM 2025; a manuscript describing the tool is under revision at *Medical Physics*.

[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/wizrads/CollimatorCreator)
