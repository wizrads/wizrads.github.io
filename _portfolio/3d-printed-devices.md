A clinical program for patient-specific 3D-printed radiotherapy accessories: electron cutouts, non-toxic tungsten-filled photon blocks, bronze-PLA lung shields for total body irradiation, and generalized shielding for total skin electron therapy.

Each of these moved through design, dosimetric validation, and QA into routine clinical use, and is described in publications in *Frontiers in Oncology*, *JACMP*, *Practical Radiation Oncology*, and *Physics and Imaging in Radiation Oncology*.
