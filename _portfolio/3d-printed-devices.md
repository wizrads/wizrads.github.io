A clinical program for patient-specific 3D-printed radiotherapy accessories: electron cutouts, non-toxic tungsten-filled photon blocks, bronze-PLA lung shields for total body irradiation, and generalized shielding for total skin electron therapy.

I was a collaborator on this work rather than its lead. I contributed to the design, printing, and QA workflows and to getting the devices into routine clinical use, as part of a larger team at Stanford and across the collaborating institutions.

These devices moved through design, dosimetric validation, and QA into routine clinical use, and are described in publications in *Frontiers in Oncology*, *JACMP*, *Practical Radiation Oncology*, and *Physics and Imaging in Radiation Oncology*.
