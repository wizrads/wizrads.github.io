## [UW–Madison Alumni Network]({{ base_path }}/projects/uw-madison-alumni-network/)

An interactive map of Medical Physics PhD and MS graduates from the University of
Wisconsin–Madison, 2019–2026. Alumni are grouped by research specialty, which is
derived from dissertation titles, and can be viewed by cohort, timeline, or shared
research ties.

Built with [D3](https://d3js.org/), bundled into the page — it loads no external
scripts, fonts, or trackers. The "Load roster CSV" control reads a file in your
browser only; nothing is uploaded.

[Open the visualization]({{ base_path }}/projects/uw-madison-alumni-network/){: .btn .btn--primary}

*Demo. Source: department alumni directory — names, degrees, and dissertation
titles are public record.*
